Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 19 September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit websites. They help remember your preferences (such as Light/Dark mode and cookie choices) and ensure forms function smoothly.

2. The Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary: Required for the basic operation of the website, including security tokens and wizard navigation state.
  • Preference Cookies: Storing your selected color theme (`anisq-theme`) and cookie consent decision (`anisq-cookie-consent`).
  • Performance & Analytics: Anonymized metrics allowing us to understand popular portfolio projects and optimize loading speed.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can choose to disable non-essential cookies via the banner presented upon first visit, or adjust settings directly in your web browser. Note that essential booking functions may require local storage to complete.
