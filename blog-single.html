What to Wear for an Autumn Family Portrait Session in London

How to coordinate colors, textures, and layers so your family looks effortless against golden park foliage.

Autumn in London offers a magical golden warmth: amber beech leaves in Trent Park, misty mornings on Hampstead Heath, and soft, low-angled sunlight that flatters every skin tone. However, one question we receive continuously is: "What should we wear so we don't clash or look identical?"

1. Coordinate, Don't Match

The era of everyone wearing identical white shirts and blue jeans is firmly in the past. Instead, pick a core 3-to-4 color palette for your family unit. For autumn, our signature harmonious tones include:

  • Terracotta & Rust: Adds rich, earthy vibrancy.
  • Sage Green & Forest: Complements autumn foliage without distraction.
  • Oatmeal, Sand & Cream: Keeps the overall portrait light and airy.
  • Espresso & Charcoal: Adds grounding contrast.

2. Embrace Textures Over Graphic Prints

Chunky cable knits, corduroy pinafores, linen scarves, and suede boots bring visual richness and depth to the photograph without competing for attention the way large logos or neon graphics do.

3. Keep Comfort First for Children

If toddlers feel itchy in stiff collars or cold in thin layers, they will understandably resist having their photo taken. Layer comfortably with thermal undergarments and breathable wool so they can run, explore, and laugh freely.
