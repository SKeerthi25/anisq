UK GDPR & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 19 September 2026

1. Introduction and Data Controller

ANISQ LTD ("we", "us", or "our"), company number 17466151, registered at Flat 30 Webley Court, 3 Sten Close, Enfield, England, EN3 6WX, operates www.anisqphoto.co.uk. We are committed to protecting the personal data and photographic privacy of our clients in strict adherence with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact Information: Name, email address (e.g. photo@anisqltd.com correspondence), phone number, and physical postal address for print delivery.
  • Commission Details: Wedding and event dates, venue addresses, and family member details pertinent to your shoot.
  • Visual Imagery: Digital raw captures, edited photographs, video recordings, and aerial drone footage.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and cookie identifiers collected via our website.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal information under the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual Performance: To schedule shoots, deliver digital galleries, and manufacture bespoke albums.
  • Legitimate Interests: To respond to enquiries, prevent spam through honeypot mechanisms, and maintain backup vaults.
  • Explicit Consent: For displaying portfolio images on our public website or social media channels. You may withdraw or restrict portfolio model consent at any time.

4. Data Storage & Vault Security

Your raw and master JPEG files are archived on encrypted, UK/EU-compliant cloud servers for up to 5 years. We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties.

5. Your Statutory Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Controller at photo@anisqltd.com.
