Everything You Need To Know

To reserve your date, we require a 25% retainer deposit along with our straightforward signed digital photography agreement. The remaining balance is due 14 days prior to your session or wedding date. We accept direct bank transfers and all major cards.

We recommend booking 9 to 18 months ahead, particularly for summer Saturdays. However, we also welcome last-minute weekday and intimate winter wedding inquiries whenever our schedule allows.

You receive a private, password-protected online gallery. A 48-hour sneak peek of 20–30 images is sent right after the shoot. The complete collection (individually hand-retouched in high resolution) is ready within 10 to 14 business days.

Please don't worry! We design our sessions with generous buffers. We take unhurried breaks for feeds, cuddles, and snacks. Some of our absolute best images happen during the candid in-between pauses.

We assess the weather 48 hours prior. In the event of heavy rain or storms, we will reschedule to another date with zero fee, or move to our Enfield studio setting.

Yes. Every private commission includes a personal print release. You can download the unwatermarked 300DPI JPEG files and print at your preferred lab or order fine art prints directly via our integrated store.
